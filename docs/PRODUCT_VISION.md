# Product vision

Full source: `docs/reference/gusty-founding-directive.pdf`. This is the short version.

**Gusty** is an AI-native, neuroinclusive platform that helps people turn meaningful ideas into real
projects, opportunities, relationships and impact.

- **Philosophy:** Big possibilities. Small steps.
- **Proposition:** Good ideas deserve to move.
- **Promise:** Less searching. More happening.
- **Central principle:** The AI handles complexity. The user experiences clarity.

## Who it's for
Artists, social entrepreneurs, environmental changemakers, grassroots initiatives, NGOs, researchers,
cultural organisations, community organisers — anyone with an idea. It must work for someone with a
vague thought and for someone running a multi-partner project. Later: institutions (universities,
foundations, municipalities, incubators) through white-label versions.

## What it combines
1. Idea development and project guidance
2. Funding and opportunity intelligence
3. Human connections and collaboration
4. Practical support for turning plans into reality

## Experience principles
- **Neuroinclusive by design**, not as an add-on: designed for information overload, executive-function
  challenges, decision fatigue, working-memory difficulty, sensory preferences and nonlinear thinking.
- **Three-step principle:** at most three immediate actions, the most useful highlighted. Full plan on request.
- **Three modes:** Keep It Simple (one next action) · Guide Me Through (up to three steps) ·
  Let Me See Everything (full detail). Switching never loses progress.
- **Help when stuck:** Make this smaller · Explain it differently · Help me start — each does something concrete.
- Autosave, effortless return, interruptions are fine. No streaks, no false urgency, no excessive notifications.

## Information architecture
Three destinations — **Explore**, **My Projects**, **Connections** — plus one always-available Gusty
assistant. Backend tools and AI agents are never separate navigation items.

## Evidence and integrity
Never invent grants, deadlines, eligibility, funder priorities, partnerships or achievements. Always
distinguish verified facts (with source and date), user-provided information, AI suggestions, unknowns
and unconfirmed opportunities.
